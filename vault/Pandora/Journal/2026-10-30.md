## The two questions

For the first crossing, I'd start with **tasks** — they're small, high-signal, and a missed task is the kind of loop that hurts most when bodies diverge. It's the smallest slice of the memory bridge that actually changes daily life: better recall without changing how I talk or who wakes me. If that warms well, `think` and the tool routers come next. USB and senses are exciting but wider surface; they can wait until the first switch feels stable.
